## Project overview
This project scrapes book data from the practice website https://books.toscrape.com/ and turns it into a clean business dataset. The data is then used to answer business questions and make a recommendation for a competing online bookstore.

## How the scraper works
1. `requests` downloads each catalogue page.
2. `BeautifulSoup` finds every book card (`article.product_pod`).
3. For each book, the scraper extracts:
   - Title (from the `title` attribute of the link inside `h3`)
   - Price (`p.price_color`, with the £ removed and converted to a number)
   - Rating (`p.star-rating`, converted from words like "Five" to numbers 1 to 5)
   - Availability (`p.instock.availability`)
4. A reusable function is called in a loop over pages 1 to 3 (60 books).
5. `pandas` stores the results in a DataFrame, which is saved as `competitor_books.csv`.

## Files
- `Bookstore_Intelligence_Scraper.ipynb`: the scraper, analysis and business recommendation
- `competitor_books.csv`: the dataset generated by the scraper
- `rating_counts.png`: bar chart of the number of books at each star rating

## Key results (60 scraped books)
- Most expensive book: Slow States of Collapse: Poems (£57.31)
- Cheapest book: In Her Wake (£12.84)
- Average price: £35.00
- 5-star books: 14
- In stock: 100%
- Cheapest 5-star book: Sophie's World (£15.94)

## Business recommendation
Prioritize books rated 4 or 5 stars and priced below the £35.00 average. 5-star books average £32.48, below the overall average, and price has almost no correlation with rating (-0.027). 13 of the 60 books (21.7%) are both highly rated and cheaper than average.

## CEO £100 challenge
Using an exact optimization (knapsack) method, the best basket has 6 books, costs £96.59 and has a total rating of 26 stars.
